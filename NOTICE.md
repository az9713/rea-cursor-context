The Windows desktop package includes Electron45.2.1 and Chromium. The original Electron license is retained as LICENSE.electron, Chromium notices as LICENSES.chromium.html, and the project license separately as LICENSE. Source: https://github.com/electron/electron/releases/tag/v45.2.1.

The recorder observes selected reported hook payloads. It does not establish final context admission, model-internal use or complete post-compaction semantics. Its hash chain is internally consistent evidence, not an authenticated audit service; retain an expected head independently to detect complete rewrites or tail truncation.
